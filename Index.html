FOUR-DAY WORKOUT ROTATION

Chest + Biceps → Legs → Back + Triceps → Shoulders + Abs

Then one recovery day and repeat. This separates leg work from upper-body sessions and gives shoulders a buffer after chest/back. Keep sessions 45–70 minutes; progress load/reps with good form. The rotation follows workout days, not weekdays.

CONSEQUENCE SETTINGS

The app uses a progressive phone-free interval for missed mandatory quests, paired with a calm reset. Keep consequences safe, finite, and compatible with medical care.

Missed-quest protocol: phone away for the assigned interval, 5 minutes of quiet reflection, write one sentence on the blocker, then make a realistic recovery plan. Push-ups, running, cycling, or wall-staring are not used as escalating punishments. Exercise stays training, not self-harm-by-productivity.
